PRIVACY POLICY.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed when you visit the KOFINAS VISUALS website or contact us by email.

LAST UPDATED · 26 SEPTEMBER 2026
01 / WHO WE ARE

DATA CONTROLLER.

This website is operated by Stathis Kofinas under the name KOFINAS VISUALS by TRAVELFILMGR, based in Greece.

Privacy contact: kofinasvisuals@gmail.com

02 / WEBSITE DATA

MINIMAL BY DESIGN.

KOFINAS VISUALS does not currently use website analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural tracking, user accounts, newsletters or contact forms.

The website code does not intentionally store visitor information through cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage.

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages website is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes.

03 / EMAIL

WHEN YOU CONTACT US.

If you choose to contact KOFINAS VISUALS by email, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, message content and any files or other information you choose to send.

  • Purpose To respond to enquiries, communicate about potential collaborations or projects, and manage related correspondence.
  • Legal basis Taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, where applicable, and our legitimate interest in responding to and managing communications.
  • Data provided Only the information that you choose to include in your communication.
04 / SERVICE PROVIDERS

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES.

The website currently relies on GitHub Pages for hosting. Email communications are processed through the email services used by the sender and by KOFINAS VISUALS.

These service providers may process technical or communication data according to their own privacy terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

GitHub states that personal data may be processed in the United States and other countries and describes safeguards including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses for relevant international transfers.

05 / RETENTION

HOW LONG.

Information received through email is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the communication, manage a potential or active professional relationship, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Certain information may be retained for longer where necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

06 / YOUR RIGHTS

YOUR DATA. YOUR RIGHTS.

Where applicable under data-protection law, you may have rights regarding your personal data. The availability of a particular right depends on the circumstances and legal basis of the processing.

Access Request information about personal data concerning you.
Rectification Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
Erasure Request deletion where the legal requirements for erasure are met.
Restriction Request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by law.
Objection Object to processing based on legitimate interests where applicable.
Complaint You may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, including the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.
07 / CHANGES

POLICY UPDATES.

This Privacy Policy reflects the website and services as they exist at the date shown above. It may be updated if the website changes, new services are introduced, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
